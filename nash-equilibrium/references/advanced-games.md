# Mixed and sequential games

If no pure equilibrium exists, consider mixed strategies. For each proposed support, solve indifference conditions, check probabilities lie between zero and one and sum to one, and verify that excluded strategies offer no improvement. Finite games have a mixed-strategy equilibrium; pure equilibrium is not guaranteed.

For sequential games, assess credible continuation behavior, using backward induction where applicable. For incomplete information, explicit beliefs and an appropriate Bayesian model may be needed. Do not force either into an unexplained simultaneous matrix.

# Negotiate: reach an agreement worth more than walking away

Pick the lens that matches the bottleneck, read its file, and apply it. Combine at most two; say which you used and why.

| Signal in the request | Lens |
| --- | --- |
| Comparing offers, setting a walk-away point, building packages, "should I accept?" | [Interests and BATNA](references/interests-and-batna.md) |
| Drafting a reply to pushback, discovery questions, resistant counterpart, "they said yes but..." | [Tactical empathy](references/tactical-empathy.md) |
| Deal stuck despite good conversations, hidden approvers, wrong parties, sequencing, multi-party | [Deal setup](references/deal-setup.md) |

Default for a full preparation: interests and BATNA first, then tactical empathy for the conversation itself. Switch to deal setup when repeated concessions have not moved the other side.

## Boundaries

- Keep the user's private limits out of any counterpart-facing draft unless they choose to disclose them.
- Never fabricate deadlines, competing offers, or authority.
- Drafting does not authorize sending, accepting, or committing on the user's behalf.

# Manage: increase what the team and its neighbours deliver

Pick the lens that matches the situation, read its file, and apply it. Combine at most two; say which you used and why.

| Signal in the request | Lens |
| --- | --- |
| Manager's leverage, delegation, task-relevant maturity, meetings, feedback, output definition | [Managerial output](references/managerial-output.md) |
| Team falling behind, treading water, backlog and interruptions, team boundaries, sustainable delivery | [Team states](references/team-states.md) |
| First or recurring 1:1, agenda, extracting actions from 1:1 notes, choosing the support someone needs | [One-on-ones](references/one-on-ones.md) |
| Senior IC role scope, archetype, choosing consequential work, cross-team adoption, promotion case | [Staff engineer](references/staff-engineer.md) |

Diagnose with evidence before prescribing: name the limiting step or team state, then the intervention and the signal that should change if the diagnosis is right.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent mood, motives, or performance history; label interpretations.
- Keep shared notes free of unnecessary sensitive detail. Scheduling, sending, or contacting people requires authorization.

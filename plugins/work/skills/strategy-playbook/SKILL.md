# Strategize: diagnose the challenge and choose what to concentrate on

Pick the lens that matches the request, read its file, and apply it. Combine at most two; say which you used and why.

| Signal in the request | Lens |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy memo, vision doc, "is this a real strategy?", plan lacks a central challenge | [Strategy kernel](references/strategy-kernel.md) |
| Engineering strategy, recurring technical decisions needing shared defaults, tech standards, RFC policy | [Engineering policy](references/engineering-policy.md) |
| Process, product, workflow, requirements list, or architecture is bloated, slow, or over-engineered | [Simplification](references/simplification.md) |

When reviewing an existing document, lead with the most consequential broken link (diagnosis → policy → actions) and propose the smallest repair. Preserve the parts that already work.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent customer demand, competitive advantages, resources, or executive agreement; label hypotheses.
- Proposed deletions are proposals until authorized; never remove safety, security, legal, or compatibility obligations without resolving them first.

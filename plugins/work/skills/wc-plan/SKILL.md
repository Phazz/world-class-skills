# Plan: allocate scarce capacity and define how success will be recognized

Pick the lens that matches the request, read its file, and apply it. Combine at most two; say which you used and why.

| Signal in the request | Lens |
| --- | --- |
| Several initiatives competing for money, people, or time; intake, portfolio review, start/pause/stop | [Project portfolio](references/project-portfolio.md) |
| CTO or VP scope: financial plan, headcount, capacity allocation, roadmap, executive alignment, decision rights | [Engineering executive](references/engineering-executive.md) |
| Objectives, key results, goals, measurable targets, committed vs aspirational, OKR check-ins or retros | [OKRs](references/okrs.md) |

Keep the order financial envelope → allocation → roadmap → measures. OKRs execute choices; they do not supply a missing strategy.

## Boundaries

- Keep proposals separate from approved commitments, and future hires separate from capacity available now.
- Recommend; do not cancel work, assign people, or spend without authorization.

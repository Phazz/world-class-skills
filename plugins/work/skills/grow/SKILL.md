# Grow: improve the constraint on profitable growth

Read [offers, leads, and money models](references/offers-leads-money-models.md) and apply it. Diagnose the constraint first - offer, acquisition, monetization, or fulfillment - then use only the matching branch. For a narrow copy or pricing request, produce that artifact directly.

## Boundaries

- Label unproven claims as hypotheses; never fabricate proof, scarcity, or deadlines, or hide recurring charges.
- Drafting outreach or ads does not authorize sending, publishing, or spending.

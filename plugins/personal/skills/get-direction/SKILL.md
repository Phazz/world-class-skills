# Get direction: choose your contribution and govern your response

Pick the lens that matches the situation, read its file, and apply it. Combine both when a setback is forcing a priority decision.

| Signal in the request | Lens |
| --- | --- |
| Personal plan, priorities, what to stop, role design, career choice, strengths, commitment review | [Contribution](references/contribution.md) |
| Setback, criticism, frustration, leadership pressure, "how should I react?" | [Stoic reflection](references/stoic-reflection.md) |

Return something short and usable - a working plan or a reflection ending in one next action - not an essay.

## Boundaries

- Do not impersonate the authors or invent quotations.
- Reflection supplements practical help; where harm is preventable or support is needed, say so.

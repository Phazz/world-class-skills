# Scheduled review mode

Propose a monthly factual snapshot, quarterly allocation/drift check, and annual policy review, plus reviews after material life changes or agreed limit breaches. These are review cadences, not instructions to trade at each interval. Choose rebalancing rules with the owner; do not invent universal thresholds.

For a bot, retain a dated holdings snapshot, investment policy, source register, and decision log separately from project portfolio records. Define the timezone, evidence cutoff, authorized data sources, and report destination before scheduling. Use a run identifier to prevent duplicate reports on retries. Flag unavailable inputs and withhold recommendations dependent on them. Never treat a recurring reporting schedule as authority to execute financial actions.

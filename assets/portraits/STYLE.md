# World-Class Skills portrait series

Ink #181E2A on uniform warm cream #F7F3E8. Antialiasing may blend these two colours only. No shading, hatching, texture, or filled masses; tiny pupils and glasses accents permitted.

Use 20–28 confident contour gestures, 4–9 px wide at 1024 px with tapered ends. Hair and beard together receive at most six broad contour gestures, at most two interior marks; suggest curls through large scallops. No individual hairs. Facial detail takes priority over clothing. Clothing receives four open strokes maximum.

Square 1024 × 1024 PNG and 320 × 320 PNG. Crown y=90, eye line y=420, chin approximately y=750; crown-to-chin height 660 px ±5%. Head centred at x=512 with reference yaw preserved. Shoulders end in open tapered strokes by y=940; minimum outer margin 64 px. No hands or props. Preserve age, facial proportions and characteristic expression respectfully.

All 14 initial production calls used their individual reference photo. Voss's earlier style-reference draft was rejected for excess detail. Nash was regenerated to remove the reference hand, then simplified; O'Brien was edited to make the bow tie an open outline.

## Normalization and review

Generated rasters were projected onto the navy–cream colour axis, with near-paper values clamped to exact cream and dense ink clamped to exact navy. Intermediate edge values are antialiasing blends of these two colours. Hand-reviewed crown, chin and eye landmarks establish uniform head scale and eye placement without distorting facial proportions. The 320 px exports use Lanczos resampling. The contact sheet uses 160 px portrait tiles with external name labels.

The stroke counts are a creative target, not a mechanically certified count. Small differences in facial contour complexity remain to preserve likeness. Aurelius is the most interpretive subject: simplifying the curls loses some marble-bust specificity. All images were inspected together at README scale.

28 portrait PNGs validated: 14 at 1024 × 1024 and 14 at 320 × 320; all corner pixels exactly #F7F3E8. Final series-sheet.png inspected after Nash and O'Brien corrections. Licence split: eight CC BY-SA derivatives, three CC BY derivatives, three public-domain references. Photographer credits supplemented from Wikimedia Commons metadata; Aurelius credited to Marie-Lan Nguyen (2011), as listed on the source page. No existing repository files edited, no commit or push.

Remaining reservations: Aurelius loses some sculptural specificity; Nash's simplified eyes soften the reference's aged expression. Hair and stroke-count budgets are approximate art-direction constraints: the generator retains a few extra moustache and facial marks for likeness, and the series is not a mechanically uniform vector set.

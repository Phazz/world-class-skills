# Portrait licences

The portraits in this folder are original line-art illustrations derived from photographs. Each portrait is licensed individually, as listed in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md):

- Portraits derived from CC BY-SA photographs are licensed under the same CC BY-SA version as their source photograph.
- All other portraits (derived from CC BY or public-domain photographs) are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

When reusing a portrait, credit both the illustration ("World-Class Skills, github.com/Phazz/world-class-skills") and the original photographer as listed in CREDITS.md. The portraits do not imply endorsement by the people shown.

`assets/people.png` is a compilation of these portraits; each portrait in it keeps its own licence and credits as listed in CREDITS.md.

The MIT licence at the repository root does not apply to the files in this folder.
